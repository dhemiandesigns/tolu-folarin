My approach

I specialize in Emotionally Focused Therapy (EFT) and incorporate EMDR-informed approaches, Accelerated Resolution Therapy (ART), and Cognitive Behavioral Therapy (CBT) where appropriate. My approach is warm, compassionate and practical — paced to what you need, not a fixed formula.

I work with both couples and individuals, including betrayed partners and partners who have been unfaithful. My goal isn't to tell you whether to stay or go — it's to help you get clarity, whichever path you choose. You don't have to navigate the aftermath of betrayal alone. Healing is possible, and you can begin today.
