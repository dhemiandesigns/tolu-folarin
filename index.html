Questions before getting started?

Do you offer direct insurance billing?

Yes. Direct billing is available for eligible insurance plans. Coverage depends on your provider and individual plan. We can help you confirm your options before your first paid session.

Do you work with both partners?

Yes. Tolu works with couples as well as individuals who have been betrayed or were responsible for the betrayal.

Do we have to know whether we want to stay together?

No. You do not need to make that decision before beginning therapy. Sessions can help you process what happened and determine your next step with greater clarity.

Are online appointments available?

Yes. Secure online sessions are available across Saskatchewan, Alberta and Ontario, with in-person availability in Alberta.

Is the consultation free?

Yes. The initial 15-minute consultation is free and helps determine whether the service is the right fit for you.
